# Cars24 Used Car Price Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing used-car data to understand resale prices and identify factors that influence vehicle pricing.

## Objectives
- Analyze used-car prices by brand and model.
- Explore the relationship between vehicle age, kilometers driven, and price.
- Compare pricing patterns across fuel types and transmission types.
- Identify trends to support data-driven decisions in the used-car market.
